Research Foundations & Literature

Our work builds on five years of published research in offline reinforcement learning and machine unlearning. The key papers are:

Gupta et al., 2019

Relay Policy Learning

introduced the FrankaKitchen benchmark we use throughout this project.

Fu et al., 2020

D4RL

established the standard datasets and evaluation protocol for offline reinforcement learning.

Kumar et al., 2019

BEAR

the algorithm we focus on for its safety constraint, and the one that proved hardest to stabilise.

Kumar et al., 2020

Conservative Q-Learning

a widely used baseline that lowers value estimates to prevent overconfidence.

Kostrikov et al., 2021

Implicit Q-Learning

the current strongest baseline on this benchmark.

Gong et al., 2024

BAFFLE

the backdoor attack framework we use to poison our datasets.
